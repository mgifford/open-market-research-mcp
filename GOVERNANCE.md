# Governance (initial proposal)

This is an early community project with no established steering committee or institutional sponsor. Repository maintainers review changes, tests, and documentation. No claim is made of agency endorsement.

## Decisions

- Keep core data formats public and extensible, and publish major changes for review.
- Changes affecting Section 508, acquisition rules, exceptions, or security need subject-matter review and authoritative citations.
- Involve acquisition professionals and people with disabilities in usability testing.
- Document rejected designs as issues or decision records rather than silently removing feedback.
- Invite maintainers of upstream source projects to co-design adapters; do not assume they will maintain this project.

## Proposed advisory roles

Acquisition practitioner, Section 508 specialist, disability community representative, open-source governance specialist, security/privacy reviewer, and technical maintainer. These are roles to recruit, not current appointments.
