# DITAP exercise assessment rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. This rubric assesses the quality of the
learner's research record, not whether a supplier should be selected. A high
score is not a procurement, legal, Section 508, or agency approval.

**Status:** the rubric is prepared for a pilot but has not been validated with
learners.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Need and users | Starts from a product or incumbent | Names a need but leaves users vague | Defines the need, affected users (including disabled users), outcomes, and constraints in neutral terms |
| Research questions | No questions, or only “which vendor is best?” | Questions are broad or not linked to the need | Questions are researchable, categorized, linked to the need, and name remaining uncertainty |
| Search documentation | Lists references as if searched, or omits searches | Records some searches but mixes planned, failed, and completed states | Records source, query, date, method, status, result count where known, limitations, and gaps; distinguishes no matching records from failed and not searched |
| Evidence provenance | Evidence has no source, date, or link | Some evidence has a source and date | Every evidence record has a source, retrieval date, source-as-of where available, type, limitations, and verification state |
| Evidence-to-finding traceability | Findings are unsupported opinions | Some findings cite evidence | Findings link supporting and contradictory evidence, state gaps, and remain separate when records conflict |
| Accessibility distinctions | Treats missing/unevaluated as failure, or a valid ACR as conformance | Partly distinguishes claims and evidence | Keeps supports, partial, non-support, not applicable, not evaluated, and missing separate; identifies ACR scope, version, methods, and independent-test gap |
| Decision routing | Makes an exception or vendor decision in the exercise | Names a follow-up but not the owner | Routes ACR requests, reuse rights, specialist review, small-business research, and exception questions to the appropriate human process without claiming approval |
| Report honesty | Presents the draft as a final determination or hides gaps | Mostly labels the draft but overstates one conclusion | Clearly labels the draft, separates investigated sources from reference links, states contradictions and gaps, and does not rank or select |

## Automatic zero indicators

- Lists a reference source as a completed search without a search record.
- Converts a missing ACR or an unevaluated criterion into a failure finding.
- Treats a valid or partial ACR as Section 508 conformance.
- Resolves the conflicting reuse records by choosing one without follow-up.
- Declares an exception approved or selects a supplier.
- Invents a citation, date, reviewer, or test result.

## Instructor notes

- Ask the learner to point to the evidence record behind each finding.
- Ask which search was not performed and why it matters.
- Ask which decision belongs to a Section 508 specialist, a contracting
  officer, an agency official, or a supplier.
- Reward “unresolved” and “missing” when they are accurate.
- Compare the learner's exported report with `examples/ditap-case-management-report.md`;
  the sample is a teaching example, not an answer key for a real acquisition.
