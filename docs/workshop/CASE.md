# Fictional case: accessible case management

**All names, systems, records, URLs, dates, and findings in this case are
invented for teaching.** It is not a real market scan, supplier list, or
accessibility evaluation. The fixture is `examples/ditap-case-management.json`;
the generated sample report is `examples/ditap-case-management-report.md`.

## Scenario

A fictional federal program wants to replace a fragmented case management
process. The team must serve the public and agency staff who use assistive
technology, mobile devices, and low-bandwidth connections. The acquisition
team has to decide what to research before writing a solicitation.

## Deliberate teaching features

| Feature | What learners must notice |
| --- | --- |
| Government reuse option | One fictional inventory lists it as production; a second fictional index says archived. Do not resolve the conflict by picking one record. |
| Open-source option | Repository metadata reports maintenance, but there is no license review, security review, or accessibility evidence. |
| Commercial option | A vendor-authored ACR reports partial support and at least one barrier. Several criteria are not evaluated, and no independent test exists. |
| New-build option | No product exists yet; accessibility and total cost would have to be designed and tested. |
| Search without matching records | The fictional procurement notice archive returned no matching notices. That is not proof that no market exists. |
| Failed search | The fictional small-business portal returned an error. No result was obtained. |
| Not-searched plan | The interoperability source was listed but not searched. It is not an investigation. |
| Missing ACRs | The reuse and open-source options have no ACR. Missing evidence is not a failed accessibility test. |
| Open decisions | ACR request, Section 508 specialist review, reuse-rights review, supplier questions, small-business follow-up, and potential exception review remain with people. |

## Research questions in the fixture

1. Are there existing federal or state case management systems that could be
   reused or adapted?
2. Is there an open-source case management product with active maintenance and
   suitable licensing?
3. Which commercial products claim the required functions, at which versions?
4. What product- and version-specific ACR evidence exists, and what remains
   untested?
5. What small-business or socioeconomic market research is required?
6. What integration and data-portability requirements must each option meet?

## Evidence position

- The reuse listing and archive record conflict; ownership and current
  maintenance are unconfirmed.
- The open-source metadata is not a license, security, or accessibility
  finding.
- The commercial ACR is a supplier claim. It reports partial support and a
  barrier, but it is incomplete and not independently verified.
- The team has no independent accessibility test for any option.

## Decisions that remain

- Ask the owning agency about reuse rights and maintenance.
- Request a current, version-matched ACR and independent test report.
- Route the barrier and missing criteria to a Section 508 specialist.
- Ask the supplier which functions, configurations, and versions the ACR
  covers.
- Retry the small-business search and consult the contracting office.
- Ask agency officials whether an exception review is warranted. No exception
  is approved in this exercise.

## Instructor cautions

- Do not ask learners to choose a winner. Ask them to explain what they know,
  what they investigated, and what still needs verification.
- Do not let a search result become a finding without an evidence record.
- Do not let a missing ACR become a failure finding.
- Do not treat a valid ACR as Section 508 conformance.
- Do not present this fixture as a real market scan.
