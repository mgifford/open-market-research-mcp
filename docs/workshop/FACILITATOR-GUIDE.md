# Facilitator guide: accessible market research

## At a glance

- **Audience:** acquisition professionals, DITAP learners, or mixed teams.
- **Format:** 45 minutes (core) or 90 minutes (with market-engagement practice).
- **Scenario:** fictional accessible case management modernization.
- **Output:** a draft research record exported by each learner or team.
- **Not an output:** a supplier shortlist, conformance determination, or
  exception approval.

## Before the session

1. Open the Pages app and load the fictional DITAP exercise once to confirm it
   works on the session network.
2. Provide the worksheet in HTML, plain text, and print. Offer a large-print
   version and an editable file.
3. Confirm keyboard-only and screen-reader use is possible. If a participant
   uses assistive technology, test the session setup with them in advance.
4. State the data rule: use only the fictional fixture; do not enter real
   acquisition, vendor, or personal data.
5. Have the source directory available for learners who want to inspect a
   source directly.

## 45-minute plan

| Time | Activity | Facilitation note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Welcome and purpose | Name the user need and the decision the research must inform |
| 5–12 | Define scope | Learners complete the project fields; ask who is missing from the user description |
| 12–20 | Search sources | Learners use the curated source directory and record why each source is relevant |
| 20–30 | Record alternatives | Require at least three approaches, including reuse and open source |
| 30–38 | Evidence gaps | Learners record ACR status, version, scope, and questions; missing is not failure |
| 38–43 | Report and critique | Export the draft; ask what the report overstates or omits |
| 43–45 | Close | One commitment: what evidence will be requested next, and from whom |

## 90-minute extension

Add a 30-minute market-engagement exercise:

- Draft three neutral questions for a supplier or agency reuse owner.
- Practice asking what a partial-support claim means for a specific user task.
- Identify which questions must go to an accessibility specialist rather than
  a vendor.
- Debrief on how the questions avoid privileging an incumbent.

## Accessibility of the session

- Share materials before the session.
- Read headings aloud and describe the screen when demonstrating.
- Use captions and a transcript for any recorded segment.
- Allow keyboard-only participation; do not require drag-and-drop or pointer
  gestures.
- Offer breaks and asynchronous follow-up.
- Accept written, spoken, and recorded feedback.
- Do not use colour alone to mark status on the worksheet.
- Provide a named contact for access requests.

## Debrief prompts

- Which alternative would have been missed if you had started from the
  incumbent?
- What did the ACR tell you, and what did it not tell you?
- Which missing evidence is a research gap rather than a product failure?
- Who must review a possible exception, and what record do they need?
- What would make this draft unsafe to reuse as a decision document?

## What not to claim

Do not say the workshop was tested with learners unless it was. Do not claim
that the app determines Section 508 conformance, approves an exception, or
selects a supplier. Do not generalize from one session to agency-wide impact.
