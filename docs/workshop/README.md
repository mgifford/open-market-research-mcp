# DITAP workshop materials

These materials support a short, fictional market research exercise for
DITAP Module 2, Sprint 4. They are teaching materials, not procurement
guidance or evidence of learner outcomes.

**Status (2026-11-12):** materials prepared; **no learner session or
instructor pilot has been run**. The repository must not claim that the
workshop was tested with participants, that it improves learning, or that it
meets an agency training requirement until a documented pilot is complete.

## Files

- [Fictional case](CASE.md) — the reuse conflict, open-source option, commercial
  ACR, failed and no-result searches, and open decisions.
- [Assessment rubric](ASSESSMENT.md) — evidence-traceability rubric and automatic
  zero indicators.
- [Facilitator guide](FACILITATOR-GUIDE.md) — timing, setup, accessibility,
  debrief, and what not to claim.
- [Worksheet](WORKSHEET.md) — printable learner record aligned to the five
  browser steps.
- [Pilot protocol](PILOT-PROTOCOL.md) — how to run and record a real pilot,
  including consent and assistive-technology participation.
- [Pilot report template](PILOT-REPORT-TEMPLATE.md) — the record to complete
  before calling the materials tested.

## Facilitation stance

- Start from users and outcomes, not a product.
- Require government reuse and open-source alternatives to be considered.
- Treat ACR claims as claims, missing evidence as missing, and exceptions as
  agency decisions.
- Do not produce a vendor ranking, a conformance conclusion, or an approval.
- Ask learners to name what they cannot verify and who should verify it.

See [DITAP alignment](../DITAP.md) for the existing learning outcomes and
rubric.
