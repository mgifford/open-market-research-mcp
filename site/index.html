Key terms and how this tool treats them
Market research vs. market intelligence
Market research is a deliberate process of defining questions, searching sources, and recording evidence. Market intelligence is ongoing information gathering. This tool records the research you performed; it does not continuously monitor markets.
Government software reuse
Adapting software already developed by a government body. Listing or finding code does not establish reuse rights, maintenance, security, or fitness.
Supplier vs. software product
A supplier is an organization; a product is a specific offering and version. Evidence should identify which one a claim is about.
Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR)
A report of an author's accessibility claims, usually mapped to a catalog. An ACR is not an independent test and a valid ACR is not a Section 508 conformance determination.
Vendor claim vs. independent evaluation
A supplier claim describes what the supplier says. Independent testing is evidence produced separately. Keep them distinct in the record.
Best Meets and Undue Burden
Section 508 exception concepts that require the agency's authorized process. This tool can help assemble questions and evidence; it cannot approve an exception.
Total cost of ownership
Acquisition, integration, operation, maintenance, accessibility remediation, exit, and other lifecycle costs. A purchase price alone is not total cost.
Evidence provenance
The source URL, retrieval date, source-as-of date, limitations, and verification state that let another person retrace a claim.

05

Review accessibility evidence

Record requirements, ACR scope, claims, and independent evidence separately.

Missing is not failed, and a valid ACR is not conformance

If an ACR is missing or a criterion is not evaluated, record that as missing information. Do not convert it into a failed test. A supplier-authored ACR is a claim; independent testing and agency review are separate. Use GSA Section 508 Buy/Sell, ART, the ACR Editor, and OpenACR rather than duplicating them.

Evidence checks

  • Accessibility: Determine applicable Section 508 criteria with GSA ART, and examine product-specific ACR claims.
  • Independent checks: Plan testing of critical tasks, including assistive technologies, where appropriate.
  • Exceptions: Use the GSA exceptions guide and agency approval process. This app cannot approve an exception.
  • Digital first: Check applicability of digital-first public experience guidance.
  • Reuse: Investigate government software indexes, SHARE IT metadata, open source, and commercial offerings.
06

Document findings and decisions

Attach evidence to claims, record contradictions, and name the decisions that still need a person.

What is evidence, what is a finding, and what is a decision?

Evidence is a source-backed record with a URL and retrieval date. A finding is a plain-language conclusion linked to evidence; if it has no evidence, it is an unresolved question or assumption. A decision is an action for a human, such as requesting an ACR or routing an exception question. None of these is an agency approval.

What this starter does not do

It cannot search live procurement records, discover ACRs automatically, assess conformance, determine exceptions, or decide which supplier to choose. The browser does not parse ACR files; a local OpenACR YAML import prototype exists in the repository CLI and MCP server, for human review only. These integrations require further development and responsible validation.

Optional local MCP, local-LLM setup, and AI guidance are on About this tool and developer options. The research workflow itself requires no installation.

Source code and technical documentation are included in the GitHub repository. This prototype is independent of GSA, DITAP, and 1102tools.
